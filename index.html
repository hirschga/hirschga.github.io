ACE Bounty Hunter

An AI-assisted tool for Glenn Hirsch to organize software bounty opportunities, track contributions, and manage related correspondence.

What ACE is designed to do

  • Identify and assess software bounty opportunities on GitHub.
  • Track issues, pull requests, maintainer feedback, and follow-up tasks.
  • Read relevant Gmail correspondence, prepare and send authorized replies, and update message labels or read status.

Gmail integration is being configured. This website does not indicate that the connector or unattended processing is operational.

Personal use and authorization

ACE is maintained for its operator’s own use. It is not currently offered as a public email service and this website has no sign-up or mailbox connection form. Gmail access requires the account owner’s Google OAuth consent.

Financial transactions and wallet actions require the operator’s separate approval.
