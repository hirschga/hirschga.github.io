Privacy Policy

This policy describes the permitted data practices of ACE Bounty Hunter, a personal application operated by Glenn Hirsch. Gmail integration is being configured; access begins only after the account owner authorizes it.

Information accessed and purposes

ACE requests the Google Gmail gmail.modify permission. This permits reading messages and threads, composing and sending messages, and changing labels and read status. The permission applies across the authorized mailbox; Google does not limit it to bounty senders. ACE’s intended processing is limited to software bounty opportunities, related correspondence, and necessary operational messages.

Relevant information can include sender and recipient addresses, subjects, message text, timestamps, message and thread identifiers, labels, and linked issue or pull-request details. OAuth credentials are used to authenticate API requests. This integration does not request access to Drive, Calendar, or Contacts.

Use and sharing

Information is used to assess opportunities, track actions, prepare or send authorized replies, avoid duplicate responses, and diagnose operational failures. Relevant message content may be processed by the AI service providers configured for ACE, solely to provide these functions. Cloud hosting providers may process operational data on the operator’s behalf.

Authorized replies disclose their contents to the intended recipients. A reply sent to a GitHub notification address may appear publicly on the associated issue or pull request. Unrelated mailbox contents and authentication credentials must not be included in public submissions.

ACE does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. ACE Bounty Hunter’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage, retention, and safeguards

The public information website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Mailbox contents, OAuth client secrets, and access or refresh tokens are not intended for this public repository. Operational records and credentials are kept separately in the operator-controlled application environment. Access must be restricted to the operator and the services needed to run ACE.

Relevant correspondence records and action history may be retained while needed for ongoing opportunities, follow-up, dispute resolution, or operational diagnosis. Retention is managed by the operator; no automatic deletion interval is represented here. Records that are no longer needed should be deleted. Deleting ACE’s local records does not delete messages already held by Gmail or external recipients.

Your control

The account owner may stop ACE, revoke Google access through Google Account third-party connections, and request deletion of ACE-held data by contacting hirschga@gmail.com. Revocation stops future authorized API access; it does not automatically erase previously retained records or recall sent messages.

Website visitors

These static pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking cookies, or forms. GitHub may process visitor technical information as the hosting provider under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes and contact

Changes to data practices must be reflected in this policy before they are put into use. The effective date identifies the current policy. Questions or deletion requests: Glenn Hirsch, hirschga@gmail.com.
